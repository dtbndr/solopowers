# Final Verification Blocker

A failed full Final verification is not a smoke finding and cannot be waived.

## Record the blocker and establish recovery state

Record it in `<workspace>/end-smoke-report.md`, creating that report if absent, and mark it `ACTIVE`. Preserve each recovery attempt as a separate history entry with its failed command/result and exact failing revision, linked repair/review range, smoke results, and rulings; retain prior entries. The latest `ACTIVE` failure identifies the current attempt and its applicable repair range; older ranges remain historical and do not govern its `HEAD` guard. After full Final verification passes, if prior smoke exists, record `VERIFICATION PASSED — SMOKE REVALIDATION PENDING` with passing revision, command/result, and the complete set of smoke checks still outstanding from prior smoke plus checks newly invalidated by the repair, less only valid current passes and named waivers, before running any of them. If an initial blocker is pending when the smoke reaches an acceptance-only cap outcome, preserve the cap rulings and marker route while resolving verification; blocker precedence forbids either success write until current full Final verification passes and all required smoke obligations are durably passed or explicitly waived. Then write/reconcile the applicable paired outcome and mark the blocker `RESOLVED` only after both records are durable. If the active recovery originated in an `End: final review fix wave`, record the continuation, original wave base, and single re-review dispatch state in this same blocker report before dispatch. Record the exact reviewed range and outcome there before proceeding; on restart reconcile that state and never dispatch a second time if dispatch/outcome may already have occurred. If dispatch state or exact coverage is uncertain, stop and ask. After validating the accepted repair tip and passing Final verification, if the saved state is undispatched, hand back to the one re-review over original wave base..current `HEAD` without adding a progress/log marker. If dispatch is already recorded, recover that same review without redispatch; if its outcome is recorded, reuse the saved reviewed range/outcome and continue remaining smoke/verification bookkeeping rather than returning to dispatch. Preserve smoke obligations and their pending state in the report; do not claim them passed, resolved, or waive them merely by dispatching the re-review. A clean re-review is not sufficient to resolve the blocker or finish: complete all required smoke obligations, require current Final verification to pass, and mark the blocker `RESOLVED` only after their outcomes are durable. If smoke then requires another code change, stop for operator direction; do not exceed the one re-review or finish at an unreviewed tip. Apply this union rule on every recovery attempt, including same-tip recovery after another verification failure. If this is the first Final verification before any smoke run, record `VERIFICATION PASSED — INITIAL SMOKE PENDING`; this is a handoff, not an empty successful smoke outcome. Resume the normal initial-smoke workflow from that checkpoint after validating `HEAD` and full verification, without repair review or revalidation. For initial pass or skip, the live closeout and resume route here before writing either success record. This procedure owns the paired smoke outcome writes for initial pass/skip and ordinary post-fix pass: `Smoke: passed — <one-liner>` in `log.md` with `End smoke: passed` in `progress.md`, or `Smoke: skipped (agent-verified)` in `log.md` with `End smoke: skipped` in `progress.md`. After post-fix recovery, every non-acceptance-only pass outcome, including mixed acceptance and code-fix rulings, uses the ordinary pass pair. For an acceptance-only cap outcome (all current cap findings accepted without code changes), preserve that route instead: after full Final verification passes and all required obligations pass or are explicitly waived, return to the acceptance-only closeout in [fix-loop.md](fix-loop.md#end-smoke-variant) to write/reconcile `Smoke: passed — <one-liner>` and `End smoke: passed (rulings accepted)`; the one-liner must name waived checks, not imply they passed. Mark the blocker `RESOLVED` only after this pair is durable. After required checks are durably recorded as passed or applicable named rulings, and current full Final verification is durably recorded, write/reconcile the pair and then mark the blocker `RESOLVED`; do not require the pair before entering this procedure. On resume, reconcile either member of the pair by adding only the missing write, never duplicating it; resolve only after both outcomes and verification are durable. For either recorded initial outcome (pass or skip), reconcile the paired log/progress records with the durable passing verification before dispatching any smoke check; complete the pair and resolve the pending blocker when both are established. If neither outcome is recorded, continue the initial-smoke workflow and dispatch only outstanding checks. Persist each initial check's revision and result; on resume reuse valid results, reconcile any recorded failure into its finding/ledger transition before proceeding, and request/run only outstanding checks. Preserve the history and record each check's result. If the set cannot be read from the checkpoint, reconstruct it from durable prior smoke evidence and the repair's impact before proceeding; if that cannot be established, stop and ask. Keep this pending state durable across crashes and resume required smoke revalidation before marking `RESOLVED`. On resume, `ACTIVE` blocks smoke success. `INITIAL SMOKE PENDING` resumes the normal initial-smoke workflow only after current full Final verification passes and `HEAD` matches its recorded revision, unless a newer durable smoke checkpoint or fix tip exists; that checkpoint supersedes the handoff for `HEAD` and routes through its smoke resume row. Before any pending revalidation check is run or its evidence accepted, validate `HEAD` against the applicable recorded repair tip; if no repair tip exists, use the pending record's passing revision as the guard. A newer valid smoke checkpoint supersedes either guard; otherwise stop and ask. `SMOKE REVALIDATION PENDING` normally routes to outstanding authorized checks; however, before any failure/escalation or code-fix handoff, reconcile whether this report contains a final-review continuation whose single re-review was already used. If so, preserve the recorded failure and cap rulings but stop for operator direction before any code-changing dispatch; do not enter a smoke fix series or verification repair. Non-code reconciliation and authorized check execution may continue. Otherwise, if the pending check has a recorded failure or a cap ruling directs code changes, hand off to the corresponding failed/escalated or authorized fix-series route in `resuming.md` without rerunning that check. Preserve the pending obligation and blocker record across the handoff. Ambiguous or incomplete status requires stopping to ask.

## Recover verification

If no code change is needed, resolve the cause within existing authorization and rerun full Final verification. Record the failing revision as the same-tip checkpoint; on resume, `HEAD` equal to that revision resumes this recovery without a repair review only when no committed repair range has been recorded. Once a repair tip exists, require `HEAD` at that tip for scoped review or stop and ask; do not fall back to the old failing revision or repair base.

## Repair and review

If code changes are needed, first inspect any final-review continuation in this report. Once its single final re-review has been dispatched or completed, stop for operator direction before dispatching any further code-changing verification repair; do not advance beyond the reviewed tip without authorization. Same-tip recovery (no code change) remains allowed. Otherwise, record the intended repair base before dispatch. Dispatch a fix implementer (the controller must not edit code). Require covering checks and independent scoped review of the exact committed `base..tip`. Record the range before dispatching review and save its outcome before relying on it. Unresolved findings or evidence gaps block progress; if repair cannot be accepted, stop for operator direction, not a waiver.

## Resume and revalidate smoke

On resume, inspect this blocker before the smoke table's tip guard. While `ACTIVE`, validate `HEAD` against the latest attempt's recorded failing revision or its repair base/tip. When Final verification fails again after a smoke fix, record that fix range and new failing revision as a new current attempt; validate against that attempt, not an older repair tip. A newer smoke checkpoint cannot otherwise supersede this guard. Once verification repair is accepted and revalidation is pending, the latest applicable smoke checkpoint may supersede the repair tip for `HEAD`; retain older checkpoints for round budget and rulings. At the recorded repair base with no tip, reconcile dispatch state and resume or redispatch the same repair from that base only if no dispatch is outstanding; if dispatch status is uncertain, stop and ask rather than duplicate it. At a recorded repair tip, resume scoped review or full verification as applicable. If a repair tip exists, `HEAD` at the base or original failing revision is a mismatch and requires stopping to ask. If commits exist without a trustworthy recorded range, or `HEAD` matches neither the applicable checkpoint nor an allowed newer smoke checkpoint, stop and ask; never infer a range or accept an unreviewed tip.

When returning to smoke, preserve rulings and unresolved cap failures. A waiver applies only to its named smoke check. While revalidation is pending, carry forward the complete required-check set across every recovery attempt and recompute after each subsequent code change: include every applicable check invalidated by the repair or later fix, plus checks still outstanding from initial smoke. A saved pass from an earlier tip may satisfy a check at the current repair tip when the recorded repair/change scope does not invalidate that check and its result remains applicable; otherwise rerun it. A saved pass satisfies a check only when its revision and applicable change scope are established, and never drop an unattempted check. Reuse durable passing results for unaffected checks rather than repeating them; run only outstanding or invalidated checks; before any external or side-effecting action, require explicit current-session authorization for that specific action as defined in [end-smoke.md](end-smoke.md). Saved authorization does not carry across sessions. Record each result durably. Before dispatching any revalidation check, apply the same cap gate as the resume path: inspect the current applicable cap state and findings across the full required-check set, not only checks pending revalidation. If any current cap finding remains unruled, re-present it and wait; dispatch no checks. Historical/superseded escalations do not block a newer authorized fix series. Preserve cap budget and rulings: never rerun an unruled cap failure. If a ruling directs a fix, hand off to the authorized cap fix series, retain the pending revalidation entry and do not mark it resolved. After the fix is independently review-clean, rerun that check and record its result before resolving the obligation. A failed authorized revalidation must first be durably recorded with check, revision, and result, and materialized as the corresponding actionable finding in `<workspace>/end-smoke-findings.md`; then append the corresponding `End smoke: failed` checkpoint, or cap escalation if the budget is exhausted, before any restart. On resume, validate `HEAD`, then reconcile all recorded failed results together. Define the group by the pending checkpoint's recorded check set and applicable revision; materialize missing findings for those checks and append one grouped failure/escalation transition only if no ledger entry already records that group. If group membership or an existing entry's coverage is ambiguous, stop and ask rather than append. For crash-safe deduplication, the durable failure record must identify the exact grouped ledger transition; if that association is absent, do not guess which line covers the group—stop and ask. Preserve the originating round/budget; never append consecutive per-check transitions or rerun checks during reconciliation. Preserve historical failures. Reconcile only recorded failures that lack their own actionable finding or grouped ledger transition, without rerunning them. Before interpreting retained failure results or rulings for routing, select the current authorized series and its newest valid fix-round checkpoint. A newer valid fix-round checkpoint, even if not CLEAN, governs resumption of its already-dispatched fix series and takes precedence over historical failures/escalations and the original directed-fix ruling; do not route back to that older failure or redispatch round 1. The ruling authorized that one series only: it does not waive a check or authorize another series if the check fails at the new cap. Independent CLEAN is required only before authorized smoke reruns. Retain rulings/waivers and round budget. On resume, first validate the applicable `HEAD`, then apply the grouped reconciliation rule above to any recorded failures missing findings or a ledger entry; do not write a duplicate transition when the existing entry covers that exact check group and revision. An existing failure/escalation checkpoint takes precedence over blocker-first dispatch and resumes that route without replaying the failed check. Preserve the pending obligation and cap budget. Keep the blocker pending through any authorized fix series. A failure or handoff alone never resolves the obligation. Once every required check has a durable passing result on the applicable revision or an explicit operator ruling disposes of that named check, require a current applicable full Final verification pass; reuse an established pass while applicable to the current revision and verification conditions, and rerun only if stale or unestablished. If verification fails, record the new command, result, and exact revision and return the blocker to `ACTIVE`, retaining smoke results, rulings, and budget. Only after verification passes and its result plus corresponding smoke outcome are durably recorded may the blocker become `RESOLVED`. On restart before that single transition, remain in pending recovery and reuse valid saved passes. On restart in this interval, validate `HEAD`, reuse durable passing results still valid at that revision, and resume only the missing Final verification or smoke-success checkpoint; never replay completed operator-dependent checks. A verification repair grants no additional smoke round and overrides no pending cap ruling.
